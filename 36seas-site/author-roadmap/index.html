The book-to-launch
roadmap.

Six stages. One next step at a time.

Save this page or print it as a PDF. Use the prompts below to turn your ambition into a working plan.

01 / Outline

Give the idea a spine.

Define your reader, your promise, and a chapter-by-chapter direction before you draft.

02 / Write

Build a writing rhythm.

Bring your ideas or manuscript into First Mate. Review suggestions and decide what belongs in your voice.

03 / Edit

Make the book stronger.

Work through structure, continuity, clarity, and line-level revisions. Human editing is a separate service.

04 / Design

Create a world readers recognize.

Explore visual references and cover directions. Review typography and final files before publication.

05 / Publish

Choose your route.

Prepare your book for self-publishing, or seek separate, selective consideration from the 36Seas imprint.

06 / Market

Start the reader conversation.

Plan your positioning, reader list, launch assets, outreach, and follow-through. Sales and rankings vary.

Your next seven days.

My reader: Who is this book for, and what do I want them to feel or learn?

My promise: What will my book offer in one clear sentence?

My current stage: Which of the six stages needs my attention now?

My next action: What is one task I can finish this week?

My writing time: When will I work, and what will count as progress?

My launch foundation: Where will interested readers find me and hear about the book?
