Before you dive in.

Can I type into the workbook?

Yes. The PDF includes 21 fillable text fields. Download it, open it in a compatible PDF reader, and save your completed copy. You can also print it and write by hand.

What happens after I sign up?

Brevo sends a confirmation email on behalf of 36Seas. Click the confirmation link to join the author list and open your workbook download page. Check spam or promotions folders if the email does not arrive.

Do I need First Mate to use it?

No. Use the workbook with your existing writing process. First Mate is available when you want to bring the plan into your author workspace.
