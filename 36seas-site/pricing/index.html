One writing balance

Shared, not multiplied.

Basic shares 70 credits between Ideas and Manuscript. Paid plans share their monthly writing allowance across Ideas, Manuscript and Write. Your own typing does not consume credits; assisted actions use credits, not a per-word or per-page price.

More than the draft

Build the complete book.

Paid plans include the Bible story-reference workspace, Visualize, AI Review and Format within their limits. AI writing in the story workspace uses the shared writing balance. Human reviews, editing and publishing services are priced separately.
