Can I self-publish with First Mate?

Yes. Use the studio independently of the 36seas imprint. Paid plans include formatting exports within their allowances. You manage final file checks, publishing accounts, and your release.

Does a subscription include publication?

No. First Mate membership covers software and the usage allowances in your plan. Human services and publishing review are separate purchases. Imprint publication requires editorial approval, commercial fit, and a separate agreement.

What does the $99 manuscript review cover?

The launch offer covers final review of one finished manuscript and consideration for publication. The standard price is $199. Contact us before purchasing to confirm timing and the feedback you will receive. Acceptance, distribution, sales, and a publication date are not guaranteed.

Are human editing, audio, and trailers included?

Human evaluation, editing, ghostwriting, and managed production are separately priced services. Audiobook and video generation are coming soon and are not currently available membership benefits.

What happens to my manuscript if I stop paying?

You keep access to manual editing, reading, and history. Paid assistance and formatting depend on your current plan and allowances. Your creative decisions remain yours; any imprint publishing rights are defined in a separate agreement.

Compare the activities you actually need: assisted writing, image exploration, AI reviews, formatting exports, and submission preparation. A higher tier increases capacity; it does not change the imprint’s selection decision.

Three distinctions worth keeping.

  • Manual typing does not consume writing credits. Assisted writing shares a balance across the applicable tools.
  • AI review is a software allowance. Human evaluation and editing are separate professional engagements.
  • Submission preparation is not paid manuscript review. Review is not guaranteed publication.
