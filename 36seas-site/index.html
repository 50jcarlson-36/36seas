Before you begin

A clear answer for your next step.

Can I use First Mate without publishing with 36 Seas?

Yes. First Mate is an author studio you can use independently. A membership does not submit your manuscript, require you to use the imprint, or guarantee publication.

What does the $99 publishing review cover?

The launch offer is a one-time payment for final review of one finished manuscript and consideration for publication by 36 Seas. It is separate from studio membership. Publication requires editorial approval, commercial fit, and a separate agreement.

Does paying mean my book will be accepted?

No. Payment is for review and consideration. It does not guarantee acceptance, distribution, sales, or a publication date. If your book is not accepted, you can continue using First Mate independently.

Are editing, covers, and trailers included in my membership?

Membership provides studio tools and the capacity listed for your plan. Human evaluation, editing, ghostwriting, and managed production are separate services. Final video and audiobook generation are planned add-ons and are not currently available. Check the pricing page and the quoted scope before purchasing.

Do I keep my rights?

Using First Mate does not transfer ownership of your manuscript. If you choose publication with 36 Seas, any publishing rights and obligations are set out in a separate agreement for you to review before signing.

Is 36 Seas a vanity press?

36 Seas offers paid author software and a paid manuscript review, with a selective publishing path. Paying for software or review does not buy acceptance. Any publication commitments, services, costs, and rights must be defined in a separate publishing agreement.

How is First Mate different from a general chat tool?

First Mate organizes your manuscript and story references in an author workspace, with tools for writing, revision, and book preparation. The focus is working on one connected book project and keeping creative decisions with you.

How long does review take, and what feedback will I receive?

A fixed turnaround and feedback format are not specified here. Contact 36 Seas to confirm the delivery scope and timing for your manuscript before purchasing.
