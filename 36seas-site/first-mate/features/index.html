01 / Ideas & manuscript

A place for the work in progress.

Ideas and Manuscript give you a place to capture, develop, and revise. Basic includes 70 one-time shared writing credits. After those are used, manual editing, reading, and history remain available. Paid plans add a monthly allowance and access to the advanced writing workflow.

Credits are shared across the applicable writing tools; each area does not receive a separate copy of the balance. Keep the displayed action cost and current plan limits in view when choosing assistance.

02 / Story reference

Keep the larger context nearby.

The Bible workspace on paid plans organizes characters, settings, and continuity. It is a reference for the manuscript rather than a substitute for reading it. Update the details when the story changes so your notes and pages continue to describe the same world.

For nonfiction, a clear project brief and organized manuscript can serve a similar editorial purpose: hold onto the reader, the central promise, and the decisions that shape the work. Verify factual claims and source material yourself.

03 / Visualize

Develop a visual direction.

Paid plans include image generations for cover concepts, character portraits, and scenes. Start with what the image should communicate: genre, tone, audience, and the part of the story you want a reader to feel.

A generated image is one component of a book’s visual preparation. Review text, typography, proportions, and final production specifications. Additional image packs are available through app billing.

04 / Review & format

Prepare with a clear division of responsibility.

Included AI reviews can inform revision. Formatting exports prepare downloadable book files and available assets within the plan allowance. Review the output; no tool can promise that an external retailer will accept every submission.

Submission preparation helps organize materials. It does not include the separate paid manuscript review or publication. Audio and video generation are coming soon and are not active membership benefits. Human editing, evaluation, ghostwriting, and managed production are separately scoped.
