01 / Direction

Give the idea a reader.

Before you ask how many chapters to write, ask who the book is for. A novel needs an invitation into its world. A professional book needs a problem, promise, or perspective a reader can recognize. Put that intention into a short working brief.

Choose a small first deliverable: a premise, a synopsis, or a provisional chapter map. The point is to make the next writing session concrete. Your structure should help you discover the book, not prevent the discoveries that improve it.

02 / Draft

Write something you can work with.

Start with an idea or bring existing pages into First Mate. Keep your manuscript near the context that shapes it: characters, settings, arguments, evidence, and unresolved questions. Separate a note for later from a change you want to make now.

Assisted writing can propose a next move. Read it as a proposal. Keep, revise, or reject it according to your voice and intention. Your own typing does not consume writing credits; assisted actions use your plan’s shared balance.

03 / Revision

Work from the large questions to the small ones.

Revisit the shape of the book before polishing every sentence. What has the reader been promised? Where does the story or argument lose direction? Which parts repeat work already done elsewhere?

AI review can help you examine the manuscript, but it is not a human editorial engagement. If you want a professional evaluation or edit, discuss a separate scope that states the material, deliverables, rounds, and responsibilities.

04 / Preparation

Choose the next destination.

When the manuscript is ready for preparation, connect the visual direction with the interior reading experience. Paid-plan formatting exports help you prepare downloadable files and available assets. Check the outputs against the requirements of your chosen publishing platform.

You can publish independently, commission specific help, or pursue a separate review for consideration by 36seas. A subscription supports the work; the imprint’s editorial decision is separate.
