Before you begin

A clear starting point.

Will this make my book a bestseller?

No service can promise your book’s sales or ranking. Our resources help you plan the work: a strong manuscript, reader positioning, production, and consistent outreach.

Is the roadmap free?

Yes. The roadmap and email updates are free. First Mate paid plans and separately quoted publishing services are optional.

Do I have to publish with 36Seas?

No. You can use First Mate independently. Submission to our imprint is a separate decision and does not guarantee acceptance.
