What to work through

Find the book’s direction.

Consider your intended reader, the promise of the book, and the first outline. Use the questions to make a working decision, then revise it as you learn.

Plan the manuscript work.

Think through writing and revision as separate kinds of effort. Identify the next deliverable and the decisions or material that would help you complete it.

Prepare the publishing route.

Clarify whether you want to manage your own release, commission specific support, or seek imprint consideration. Each route has different responsibilities.

Introduce the book to readers.

Connect positioning, approved assets, a purchase destination, and launch follow-through. Plan what you can realistically maintain.

Make it a working session

One week.
One useful deliverable.

Choose the stage you are in, identify the next task, and define a result you can recognize. The workbook is a place to record a plan—not a promise of bestseller status or acceptance.
