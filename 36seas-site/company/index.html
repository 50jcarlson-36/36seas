36seas is an independent publishing company and the team behind First Mate. We bring together distinctive books, practical author tools, and a selective publishing path.

From one manuscript to a wider possibility

Built from
finishing a book.

Joshua Carlson began Meat Wagon in 2009, writing it without AI. The work beyond the draft—revision, cover design, editions, metadata, and publication—inspired First Mate.

The result is an author studio that keeps those tasks connected, with creative decisions left to the writer.
