First Mate: an author studio for drafting your book

By 36Seas Publishing · Updated September 22, 2026

First Mate brings ideas, manuscript work, and book preparation into an author workspace. Use it independently of 36Seas publishing services, with creative decisions remaining yours.

Move from an idea into manuscript work

Capture ideas and work on your manuscript on mobile or desktop. Your own typing does not consume writing credits; assisted actions use the shared writing balance described in your plan.

Keep book preparation connected

Paid plans include the Bible story-reference workspace, Visualize, AI Review, and Format within their listed allowances. AI review is not human editorial review, and preparing an export is not a guarantee that a publishing platform will accept it.

Understand what is separate

Human evaluation, editing, ghostwriting, and managed production are separately priced services. Audiobook and video generation are not currently available membership benefits. See the current plans, allowances, and service distinctions before choosing.

Can I use First Mate without publishing with 36Seas?

Yes. Membership does not submit your book to the imprint or guarantee publishing acceptance. You can manage your own publishing path or discuss a separate service agreement.
