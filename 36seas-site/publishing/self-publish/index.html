01 / Choose your route

Decide where the book is going.

Choose your intended retailer, format, and edition before treating an export as final. Print interiors, covers, and digital editions have different requirements. Check those requirements at the platform where you intend to publish.

First Mate’s export path supports authors who manage their own release. It does not automatically open a retailer account, upload your book to Amazon, or create an imprint publishing agreement.

02 / Prepare the files

Review the book as a reader will encounter it.

Check chapter order, headings, front matter, back matter, and any links. Inspect your interior on the reading surfaces that matter for the chosen edition. For print, check the relationship between trim size, margins, images, and final page count.

A cover wrap must match the intended production specifications. A strong front-cover concept is not, by itself, proof that the spine and back cover are ready. Recheck your files after changes that affect pagination.

03 / Understand allowances

Choose the capacity for your process.

Standard includes 3 formatting exports per month, Pro 7, Premier 15, and Ultra 40. Formatting requires an active paid membership. Confirm the formats and assets available for your project inside the app before planning the final release.

Leave room for iteration in your schedule. Export, inspect, correct, and inspect again. Your book benefits from a deliberate final pass; finishing a file is different from confirming its suitability for the destination.

04 / Manage the release

Keep a record of what you approve.

You manage your publishing accounts, final uploads, edition information, and release settings. Confirm your metadata and the purchase destination before directing readers there. Keep copies of the exact files used for each edition.

If a task needs specialist attention, discuss a separately priced service. If you want 36seas imprint consideration, use its separate manuscript review path. Neither choice is required to use First Mate independently.
