01 / Scope

What you are purchasing.

The launch offer is $99 USD for final review of one finished manuscript and consideration for publication by 36seas. The listed standard price is $199. It is a one-time service, separate from First Mate membership.

Contact 36seas before purchasing to confirm the review timing and the feedback you will receive. Do not assume an edit, rewrite, production package, or publication commitment is included in the review price.

02 / Preparation

Choose the manuscript you want reviewed.

Prepare your finished manuscript, synopsis, author biography, intended reader, and relevant comparable titles. Review the submission guidelines so the team can understand both the work and its context.

Choose the manuscript in the application before checkout. Creating an account or saving an offer does not purchase the review. Review the amount shown at checkout before you pay.

03 / Outcome

Review is not acceptance.

Payment submits the selected manuscript for final review. It does not guarantee acceptance, distribution, sales, or publication on a particular date. Publication requires editorial approval and commercial fit.

If a publishing relationship is offered, it requires a separate agreement. That agreement establishes costs, services, responsibilities, and rights. Any distribution or showcase commitments must be defined there.

04 / Orders & questions

Know where to follow up.

The application provides Publishing orders in the dashboard for the order and its status. Keep the order reference available when asking about an order or refund.

Read the publishing terms and refund policy before purchasing. If you are unsure whether the manuscript is ready or whether the review is the right service, start with a question rather than making assumptions about the scope.
