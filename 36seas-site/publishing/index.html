Independent publishing: choose your path

By 36Seas Publishing · Updated September 22, 2026

A finished manuscript opens several routes. Decide what you want to manage yourself and where professional support would help before choosing software or services.

What does publishing involve?

Plan the editorial work, interior and cover preparation, release information, and publishing account responsibilities. Distribution is the route through which a book becomes available to buyers; it is not the same as editing or producing the book.
