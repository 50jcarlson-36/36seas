Turn professional expertise into a book

By 36Seas Publishing · Updated September 22, 2026

A useful professional book gives a defined reader a clearer way to understand or solve a problem. Begin with that reader before deciding how much of your expertise belongs in the manuscript.

How do I turn expertise into a manuscript?

Write a one-paragraph brief naming the audience, the problem, and the outcome the book should support. Organize chapters around the reader’s questions rather than your organization’s departments. For each chapter, identify the explanation, evidence, and example it needs.

Prepare a technical manuscript for broader readers

Define unfamiliar terms where readers first need them. Give diagrams descriptive captions, identify sources, and separate examples from general conclusions. Keep a permissions list for third-party material and decide who will review technical accuracy.

What should a publishing proposal cover?

Bring a synopsis, intended audience, approximate manuscript length, draft status, illustration requirements, and desired timing. Identify reviewers and approval responsibilities. Ask which editorial, design, formatting, and production tasks can be included and which remain with your team.
