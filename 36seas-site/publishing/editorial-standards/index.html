The 36seas field notes

A clear reason
to join the list.

Editorial consideration begins with the work, its reader, and its fit with 36seas. Selection is a decision, not a membership benefit.

On this page

VoiceReaderReadinessDecision

01 / Voice

Why should this book exist?

Show the perspective or story that makes the manuscript distinctive. A clear reason to exist can be an unfamiliar world, a well-developed argument, a deeply considered experience, or a useful framework. State it in language a reader can understand.

36seas considers fiction, narrative nonfiction, professional thought leadership, and distinctive work with a clear point of view. A category label opens the conversation; the manuscript must carry it.

02 / Reader

Who will recognize the promise?

Describe the intended audience specifically. “Everyone” makes it difficult to evaluate positioning. Comparable titles can clarify the reader’s expectations while explaining how your own book contributes something different.

An existing audience can be relevant context, but a platform description should be accurate. Present the experience, reach, or subject knowledge you actually bring rather than projecting an imagined launch.

03 / Readiness

Let the manuscript show the work.

The paid final-review offer is for one finished manuscript. Check continuity, completeness, readability, and the materials requested for consideration. If the book still needs substantial development, begin with revision or discuss a professional service scope.

Originality, voice, editorial work required, and commercial fit are part of consideration. Be ready to describe any prior publication or representation history and to discuss the material you are submitting.

04 / Decision

An agreement follows acceptance.

Review payment does not guarantee publication. Selected projects progress to a separate publishing conversation and agreement, which defines the services, costs, rights, and responsibilities.

No acceptance percentage, seasonal cohort, or limited submission window is promised here. If an actual submission window is announced, its dates and conditions should appear with the offer. Until then, evaluate the current review process on its stated terms.
