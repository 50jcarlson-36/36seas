Some legends are warnings.

Nine figures. An ancient search. A place where warning and invitation become the same thing. The search for the Fountain of Youth becomes a crossing into the strange and the shadowed.

Inside the premise

The legend

The fountain calls.

The promise of youth draws seekers toward Florida’s interior. The legend is an invitation to imagine an escape from ordinary limits. It is also a question: what has been left out of the telling?

The crossing

Some paths close behind you.

As the search moves deeper into the forest, the familiar world becomes less certain. The setting gives the journey its atmosphere: the feeling that the destination may know more about the seekers than they know about it.

The consequence

Finding is only the beginning.

The Ocala Nine brings together mystery, obsession, and the consequences of pursuing a promise. It is a book for readers drawn to dark adventure and legends that refuse to stay safely in the past.
