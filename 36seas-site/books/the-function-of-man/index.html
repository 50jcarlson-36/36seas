Inside the premise

Purpose

Choose a direction.

A meaningful direction begins with attention: to what matters, what can change, and what deserves your effort. The Function of Man invites readers to consider purpose as something to engage with in daily life.

Agency

Take ownership of the next step.

The book’s public premise centers on personal responsibility and forward motion. Rather than waiting for perfect certainty, consider the decisions available to you and the habits that make those decisions real.

Reflection

Bring the question into your life.

Readers interested in purpose, resilience, and intentional action can use this perspective as a starting point for reflection. These themes also inform Joshua Carlson’s speaking on personal agency.
