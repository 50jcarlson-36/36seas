KDP formatting: trim size, bleed, and margins

By 36Seas Publishing · Updated September 22, 2026

Choose the edition settings before laying out the manuscript. Check the final file against Amazon’s current requirements rather than assuming a single template fits every book.

What is trim size?

Trim size is the finished book’s width and height. Select it before calculating the final layout and page count.

When does an interior need bleed?

Bleed is needed when artwork reaches a trimmed page edge. Keep this interior decision separate from cover-file preparation.

How do I choose margins?

Check the current margin requirements for the page count and bleed settings. The inside margin is also called the gutter.

Amazon’s official trim size, bleed, and margin specifications are the reference for supported dimensions and current requirements.

A practical review sequence

  1. Write down the edition settings before beginning the layout.
  2. Compare those settings with the exported file.
  3. Inspect chapter openings, captions, tables, and page breaks.
  4. Review the cover and interior as separate deliverables.
  5. Resolve issues in the publishing platform’s preview before approving the edition.

Plan your preparation work

Make a short brief describing the edition, source manuscript, illustrations, and output you need. Use it when comparing production support or planning your own formatting workflow. No checklist guarantees platform acceptance.
