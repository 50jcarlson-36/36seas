Book formatting: prepare the reading experience

By 36Seas Publishing · Updated September 22, 2026

Formatting turns a revised manuscript into a readable edition. Decide which edition you are producing before choosing page dimensions or exporting files.

Print and ebook layouts need separate decisions

Plan the print interior and cover as distinct deliverables. For an ebook, review the reading experience separately instead of assuming the print file will serve every format.

Understand the print specification

Read the KDP trim size, bleed, and margins guide before preparing a paperback file. Record the edition choices you are using so the interior and cover can be checked together.

Should I format my book myself?

Consider the complexity of your material and your ability to inspect the exported edition. Tables, illustrations, references, and technical notation deserve careful review. If you request professional help, specify the editions, source files, revision scope, and final deliverables.
