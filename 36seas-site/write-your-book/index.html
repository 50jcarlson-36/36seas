First Mate / Your author journey

The book you imagine.
The next step you can take.

Start with the work in front of you. Develop an idea, strengthen a draft, prepare an independent release, or explore the imprint’s review process.

Give the idea a direction.

Define the reader, premise, and early structure. Build a short brief you can return to before the next writing session. The first outline can change as you learn what the book needs.

Build the manuscript.

Write in manageable sections. Keep your project context nearby, track unresolved questions, and preserve the decisions that make the work your own.

Revise with a purpose.

Separate structure, continuity, and sentence-level attention into deliberate passes. Use suggestions as material to assess, and commission human expertise if it would help.

Prepare the edition.

Develop the visual direction, review the interior, and check available formatting exports. Match the final files to the intended publishing destination.

Choose the publishing route.

Publish independently with your exported files, request a defined scope of professional help, or prepare a finished manuscript for separately paid 36seas consideration.

Introduce it to readers.

Connect a clear book promise with approved assets and a purchase destination. Plan the follow-through as carefully as the first announcement.
