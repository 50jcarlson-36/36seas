Four concepts, each with a literary and a direct variation. Preview the real components below. Automatic invitations use the direct variation; no random testing or tracking is active.

01 / Exit-intent reader hook

The reader invitation

Trigger. Desktop top-edge exit intent on catalog, book, or reader-editorial pages, after 20 active seconds and 20% scroll. No simulated exit intent on touch devices.

Design. A centered charcoal dialog with a real book cover on the left, warm serif headline, and one book selector. On narrow screens, text takes priority. The action leads directly to the selected Amazon listing.

Literary variation

Headline
Some stories follow you ashore.
Sub-headline
A miracle with a terrible secret. A legend with a price. Choose a story from 36seas and take the next chapter with you.

03 / Prestige curation

The 36seas imprint / Editorial consideration

Trigger. After the visitor reaches 70% scroll on Write Your Book and has spent at least 20 active seconds there.

Design. A gold-rule editorial invitation with clear review terms. No invented cohort, deadline, or acceptance rate. Finished manuscripts lead to review information; unfinished work leads to a development guide.
