This policy explains what information 36Seas Publishing collects through 36seas.com, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Privacy policy

What we collect.
How we use it.

This policy applies to the 36Seas website, reader communications, manuscript inquiries, and speaking inquiries. It does not govern third-party websites that we link to.

Information you provide

We may collect your name, email address, message, manuscript proposal, event information, and other details you choose to send when you join the reader list, request a trailer alert, submit work, or contact us.

How we use information

  • Send the book news, trailer alerts, and offers you requested.
  • Respond to publishing, rights, speaking, media, and general inquiries.
  • Review manuscript proposals and communicate about potential projects.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website and reader experience.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

Forms and service providers

Website forms are processed through FormSubmit and delivered to 36Seas. We may use service providers for hosting, email delivery, site security, or other business operations. They may process information only as needed to provide those services.

Cookies and local storage

Necessary browser storage remembers your privacy choices. Optional analytics and marketing categories remain off unless you allow them. The site does not currently load third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, this policy and the consent controls will be updated.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers, professional advisers, or authorities when reasonably necessary to operate the business, comply with law, or protect 36Seas, our readers, authors, and others.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including legal, accounting, and recordkeeping needs. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may unsubscribe from promotional email at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of certain personal information. Send requests to hello@36seas.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children

The website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

External links

Amazon, GhostUX, event providers, and other linked services operate under their own privacy policies. Review those policies before providing information to them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The date below shows the latest revision. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to hello@36seas.com.
