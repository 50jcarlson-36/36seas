Give your book
a clear introduction.

Prepare materials that explain the manuscript, its reader, and why you are the person to write it. Review the paid consideration process before purchasing.

The overviewThe authorThe materialThe next step

01 / The overview

Explain the book without making the team guess.

Prepare the working title, category, current word count, and completion status. Include a one-page overview or synopsis that communicates the shape of the work. Fiction and nonfiction need different kinds of explanation; clarity matters more than promotional language.

Describe the intended reader and relevant comparable titles. Explain the relationship between your book and those titles, including what your work contributes that is distinct.

02 / The author

Give the relevant context.

Include an author biography and the experience or platform relevant to the book. State prior publication or representation history. Explain why the manuscript belongs with 36seas.

Keep claims specific and verifiable. An accurate account of your experience is more useful than a generic promise of bestseller potential.

03 / The material

Prepare work that represents the manuscript.

The existing submission guidance requests the first three chapters or up to 10,000 words alongside the proposal. The paid final-review offer is for a finished manuscript selected through the application. Confirm the requested materials with the team if you are uncertain which stage applies.

Keep your own copies. Check chapter order, readability, and file completeness before sharing. No manuscript is uploaded or submitted by the checklist on this page.

04 / The next step

Understand what happens before paying.

Contact the team to confirm the review timing and feedback. The $99 launch review is a separate service, not a publishing package. The standard listed price is $199.

Publication remains selective and requires a separate agreement. If the manuscript is not finished, FirstMate or a separately scoped professional engagement may be a more useful immediate step.
