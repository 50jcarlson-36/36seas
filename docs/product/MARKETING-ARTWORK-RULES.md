# Marketing artwork rules

- The Meat Wagon visual-world section on the homepage and pricing page must feature the owner-supplied complete back–spine–front spread, prominently and uncropped on desktop and mobile.
- Preserve the original artwork and typography. Front-cover thumbnails elsewhere do not replace the full spread.
- Use intrinsic image proportions and a full-size link; no fixed-height crop or portrait-only column for the jacket.
- Keep generated world concepts separate and accurately captioned. Do not label the published cover as temporary AI concept art.
- A redesign must preserve this section unless the owner explicitly changes the requirement.
- Run `node scripts/check-cover-spread.mjs` when changing these sections or their shared styles.
